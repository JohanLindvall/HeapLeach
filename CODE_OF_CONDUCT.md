# Code of conduct

HeapLeach welcomes people of every background and experience level.
These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces, including maintainers.

## Working together

- Be patient with questions and give feedback that helps someone act on it.
- Discuss the code and the problem. Do not insult or attack the person.
- Respect differences in experience, identity, language and opinion.
- Accept corrections and give credit for others' work.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, credentials or
  private content without permission.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, repeated
disruption and retaliation against someone raising a concern are not
acceptable.

## Reporting a concern

Use **Report content** in the menu on an issue, pull request or comment.
Where available, choose **Report to repository admins** to alert maintainers.
For threats, abuse, sensitive reports or a concern involving a maintainer,
use GitHub's reporting channel instead.
[GitHub explains the available reporting options](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Do not repeat private information in a public issue to report a violation.
Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, explain the expected change,
warn participants, restrict interactions or block participation. The response
should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior; serious harm can
warrant immediate restrictions.

Apply these expectations consistently, explain moderation decisions when
doing so does not disclose private information, and do not retaliate against
people who report concerns.
